# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and scientifically constructive
community. Participants should be able to ask questions, report problems, and
contribute without harassment or discrimination.

## Expected behavior

- Communicate with patience and professional courtesy.
- Critique ideas, methods, and code with specific evidence.
- Welcome questions from people with different technical backgrounds.
- Respect privacy, attribution, and research ethics.
- Disclose conflicts of interest that could affect a contribution or review.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, threats, deliberate
misrepresentation of research results, and publication of another person's
private information are unacceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to `lianglu@berkeley.edu`. Project
maintainers may edit or remove contributions, restrict participation, or take
other proportionate action. Reports will be handled as confidentially as
practical.

This policy applies in the repository, issue tracker, pull requests, and other
project-managed spaces.
